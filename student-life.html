Promoting Education 5.0 through music and culture.

Detembo Re Yambiro is more than an album — it's a declaration. Produced entirely by Ndarama learners, it tackles themes that matter: drug awareness, hope, heritage, ambition, and the weight of growing up in modern Zimbabwe.

On July 10, 2026, Minister Chadzamira graced the launch, marking Ndarama's arrival not just as an academic institution, but as a cultural force. The Golden Voices Band — our school's music ensemble — brought years of practice, discipline, and passion to every note.
