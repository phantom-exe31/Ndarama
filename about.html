A timeline of excellence.

1984 Founded as part of Zimbabwe's government initiative to expand affordable secondary education in Masvingo.
Early Years The hot-sitting system is established — morning and afternoon sessions — to accommodate growing enrollment in limited facilities.
2016 A 76.25% O-Level pass rate. Praise T. Sombi scores 12 As — a school record that stands for years.
2017 Head Shirley Makausi leads efforts to mobilize resources for a new A-Level science laboratory block.
2024 A 99.4% A-Level pass rate. 42 candidates score 15 points or above.
2025 A new school bus arrives. A 100% A-Level pass rate record — the first perfect rate in school history. Elias Murisi scores a maximum 30 points in Sciences.
2026 The first student album, "Detembo Re Yambiro," launches. Infrastructure projects kick off.

Our Leadership

Portrait — Headmaster Oddy Matongo
(navy suit, classroom backdrop)
Mr. Oddy Matongo
School Head

"The results are a result of combined efforts from the learners and their teachers, and I want to thank them all for raising our school flag higher."

Under Mr. Matongo's leadership, Ndarama achieved its first-ever 100% A-Level pass rate in 2025 and launched its first music album in 2026.

Past heads include Shirley Makausi (2017), who led the mobilization for the school's science laboratory block, and earlier leaders whose groundwork shaped Ndarama's foundations.
