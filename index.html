Ndarama High School — The School of Excellence

In 1984, the government of Zimbabwe established Ndarama High School with a simple mission: bring affordable, quality education to the people of Masvingo.

Four decades later, that mission has produced scientists, artists, engineers, and leaders who serve Zimbabwe and the world.

We are a government day school. We operate on hot-sitting. We share resources. And yet — we consistently outpace schools with far greater means.

Promoting Education 5.0 through music and culture.

On July 10 2026, Ndarama made history with the launch of our first-ever music album, composed entirely by our learners from Forms 3 to 6.

Topics addressed in the album range from drug awareness to child abuse and national heritage. The launch event was held at the school grounds, and was graced by Minister Chadzamira and other delegates.

"This is not just music. This is who we are."
